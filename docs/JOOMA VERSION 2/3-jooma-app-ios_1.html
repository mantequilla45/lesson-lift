Equivalent fractions

Year 4 Maths, 50 minutes, mixed ability

Learning objective

To recognise and show, using diagrams, families of common equivalent fractions.

At a glance

0 to 8Retrieval starter on halves and quarters.
8 to 18Fold a paper strip into halves, quarters, eighths.
18 to 38Fraction wall investigation, differentiated three ways.
38 to 46Independent practice, choose your challenge.
46 to 50Plenary. True or false: 3/6 is the same as 1/2.

Differentiation

  • Working towards. Pre-printed walls, halves and quarters only.
  • Expected. Build the wall, find three pairs, record as number sentences.
  • Greater depth. Go beyond eighths and explain the relationship.

SEND adaptations

  • Sentence stem: "I know _ is equivalent to _ because _."
  • Pre-teach the word equivalent with a visual card.
  • Photograph the wall instead of writing it out.
